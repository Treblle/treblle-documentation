# Billing

The **Billing** page redirects you to Treblle's billing portal where you can manage your subscription plan, view invoices, and update payment details.

<ImageCard src="/Settings - billing.png" alt="Billing Settings" />

## What You Can Manage

### Subscription Plan
View your current plan and upgrade or downgrade as needed.

- **Free** — Limited APIs and requests
- **Team** — Extended limits, security features, SSO
- **Enterprise** — Unlimited APIs, custom SLAs, dedicated support

### Payment Details
Update your credit card or billing information.

### Invoices
Download past invoices for accounting and compliance purposes.
