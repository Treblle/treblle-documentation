# Groups

The **Groups** page lets you organize workspace members into logical groups, making it easier to manage permissions and access at scale.

<NoteCard>
Groups are available on **Team** and **Enterprise** plans.
</NoteCard>

<ImageCard src="/Settings - groups.png" alt="Groups Settings" />

## Group List

Each group displays:

- **Group Name**: The name of the group
- **Members**: Number of users in the group
- **Role**: The role assigned to this group

## Creating a Group

To create a new group:

1. Click **Create Group**
2. Enter a group name
3. Add members to the group
4. Assign a role
5. Save

## Managing Groups

- **Add/Remove Members**: Update group membership at any time
- **Change Role**: Reassign the role applied to the group
- **Delete Group**: Remove a group (members remain in the workspace with individual roles)
