# Users

The **Users** page lets you manage the people who have access to your Treblle workspace. You can invite new members, view existing users, assign roles, and remove access.

<ImageCard src="/Settings - users.png" alt="Users Settings" />

## User List

The user list displays all workspace members with the following information:

- **Name**: The user's display name
- **Email**: The user's email address
- **Role**: Their assigned role (e.g. Admin, Member, Viewer)
- **Status**: Active or pending invitation

## Inviting Users

To invite a new user to your workspace:

1. Click **Invite User**
2. Enter their email address
3. Select a role
4. Send the invitation

The user will receive an email invitation to join your workspace.

## Removing Users

To remove a user, select their row and choose **Remove from workspace**. This immediately revokes their access.
