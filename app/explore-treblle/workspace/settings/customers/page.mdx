# Customers

The **Customers** page in workspace settings allows you to manage API consumers — the external users, clients, or systems that interact with your APIs.

<ImageCard src="/Settings - customers.png" alt="Customers Settings" />

## Customer List

The customer list shows all consumers associated with your workspace:

- **Name**: The consumer's display name or identifier
- **Email**: The consumer's email address
- **APIs**: APIs this consumer has access to
- **Status**: Active or inactive

## Adding a Customer

To add a new customer:

1. Click **Add Customer**
2. Enter their name and email
3. Assign the APIs they should have access to
4. Save

## Managing Customer Access

You can update which APIs a customer can access, or deactivate a customer to revoke their access without permanently removing them.
